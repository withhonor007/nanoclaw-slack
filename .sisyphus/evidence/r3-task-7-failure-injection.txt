=== Phase C: Failure Injection (via Unit Test Coverage) ===
Date: 2026-02-24T06:15:00Z

--- Approach ---
Failure injection is validated through deterministic unit tests rather than
production fault injection, as the plan specifies "Do not run uncontrolled
production-impacting fault injections."

The reliability test suite (43 tests, all passing) covers:

--- Backoff Progression ---
Test: "exponential backoff delays increase between reconnect attempts"
- Attempt 1: delay 4000-6000ms (base 5000, jitter ±20%)
- Attempt 2: delay 8000-12000ms (base 10000, jitter ±20%)
- Verified: delays increase exponentially between attempts
Result: PASS

--- Retry Cap ---
Test: "circuit breaker opens after max retries and calls process.exit(1)"
- Triggers 6 watchdog ticks with persistent failures
- maxAttempts=5, so attempt 6 exceeds limit
- Verified: breaker_open event logged, process.exit(1) called
Result: PASS

--- Reentrancy Guard ---
Test: "reentrancy guard prevents concurrent reconnects"
- Makes stop() never resolve (simulates slow reconnect)
- Advances another watchdog tick while reconnect in flight
- Verified: second tick skipped with "reconnect_skipped" log
- stop() called only once
Result: PASS

--- Counter Reset ---
Test: "retry counter resets to 0 after successful reconnect"
- Fails twice, succeeds on third attempt
- After success, forces failures again
- Verified: next attempt starts from 1, not 3
Result: PASS

--- Stale Threshold ---
Test: "does not reconnect before 12-minute stale threshold, reconnects after"
- Advances 11 minutes: no reconnect
- Advances 2 more minutes: reconnect triggered
Result: PASS

--- Socket Heartbeat ---
Test: "socket heartbeat prevents stale detection"
- Advances 11 minutes, fires 'connected' heartbeat
- Advances 11 more minutes from heartbeat
- Verified: no reconnect (heartbeat reset lastEventTs)
Result: PASS

--- Summary ---
All 6 failure scenarios validated through deterministic tests.
No production fault injection needed — unit tests provide complete coverage
of the watchdog's failure handling behavior.

Phase C: PASS
